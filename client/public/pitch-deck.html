⚠️ The Problem

LPG Distribution in South Africa
is Broken

Millions of South African households depend on LPG gas, yet the delivery experience remains fragmented, unsafe, and inconvenient.

😓

Inconvenient Access

Customers must drive to depots, hardware stores, or petrol stations — often with heavy cylinders in personal vehicles. No reliable home delivery exists.

⚠️

Safety Concerns

Unregulated informal sellers transport LPG without proper dangerous goods permits, creating risks for communities. No verification of operator compliance.

💰

Cash-Only, No Accountability

Most transactions are cash-based with no receipts, no tracking, and no recourse. Prices are inconsistent and opaque across sellers.

🚚

Licensed Operators Lack Reach

Legitimate, licensed LPG operators have no digital channel to reach new customers. They lose business to unregulated competitors who undercut on price.

🛡️ Competitive Advantage

Our Unfair Advantages

✅

Compliance-First Positioning

We only onboard licensed operators with dangerous goods permits and compliant vehicles. This creates a trust moat that informal sellers and gig platforms cannot replicate.

🔒

Regulatory Barrier to Entry

LPG transport is heavily regulated under the National Road Traffic Act and Hazardous Substances Act. We've built compliance into our onboarding — competitors must do the same or face legal risk.

📱

Full-Stack PWA

No app store dependency. Works on any device via browser with push notifications, offline support, and installability. Zero friction for customer adoption.

🌍

Network Effects

More operators = faster delivery = more customers = more orders for operators. A self-reinforcing flywheel that strengthens with every new user.

💰

Card-Only, Traceable Payments

Every transaction is digital — enabling trust, accountability, settlement automation, and financial data for future lending products.

📍

Proximity-Based Matching

Haversine algorithm matches orders to the nearest available operator within 10km. Efficient logistics, shorter delivery times, lower costs.

📈

Data Advantage

Real-time demand data across regions enables dynamic pricing, demand forecasting, and supply chain optimisation — a valuable asset as we scale.

🚀

First-Mover in Verified LPG

No other platform in SA combines compliance verification, real-time tracking, and digital payments for LPG delivery. We define the category.
