4.Assumptions & client dependencies

  1. Compliance sign-off. Approval of client-facing journeys by Masthead / the licence holder is a client responsibility and a precondition for launch. Delays in obtaining sign-off move the delivery date, not the price. We recommend engaging your Masthead contact during Sprint 0 with the working prototype.
  2. Launch insurer panel. Integrations cover a named panel of up to 5 short-term and 5 life insurers, confirmed together in Sprint 0. Further insurers connect through the automated email lane at launch and can be upgraded to full integrations afterwards (see options below).
  3. WhatsApp verification. Meta Business Manager verification is initiated in week 1; WhatsApp go-live is contingent on Meta's approval timeline, which is outside our control.
  4. Third-party fees at cost. Usage-based fees (Meta conversation fees, document-AI processing, lead-vendor charges) are passed through at cost and are not marked up.
  5. One lead vendor. Version 1 integrates a single bought-lead vendor; additional vendors are priced separately.
  6. Access & content. You provide domain, hosting and WordPress access, brand assets, your FICA/onboarding documentation, and feedback within 3 business days at each milestone.
  7. Out of scope for v1. Native app-store apps, full rebrand, commission/accounting modules and additional product lines — available as priced options below or scoped in Phase 2.
