## Shot 1 — genre `noir`

**Video prompt**

Wide shot, 40mm anamorphic. Near-black open-plan office at 11pm, empty desks receding into darkness. Alone at a desk sits a woman in her mid-30s with deep brown skin, close-cropped natural black hair, thin gold-rimmed round glasses, small gold stud earrings, a charcoal crew-neck sweater with sleeves pushed to the elbows, calm focused expression, face and hands lit only by cool monitor glow, unreadable dashboard light reflected in her glasses. Fine cyan neural-mesh particles drift through the dark air. Over eight seconds one slow dolly push-in toward her as she exhales, leans back and rubs her eyes. Single monitor key, faint royal-blue rim from a distant window, deep blacks, oval bokeh. Moody, premium. No text, no logos, no readable screens.

**Keyframe prompt**

Cinematic still, wide, 40mm anamorphic. Near-black open-plan office, empty desks receding into darkness. Alone at a desk sits a woman in her mid-30s with deep brown skin, close-cropped natural black hair, thin gold-rimmed round glasses, small gold stud earrings, a charcoal crew-neck sweater with sleeves pushed to the elbows, calm focused expression, upright, face and hands lit only by cool monitor glow, unreadable dashboard light in her glasses. **Risks**

(1) Legible monitor/dashboard garbling into fake UI: screen content is never described, only 'unreadable dashboard light reflected in her glasses', with an explicit no-text/no-logos/no-readable-screens close. (2) Room brightening or extra practicals: 'single monitor key', 'deep blacks', noir genre. (3) Extra people: 'empty desks', 'alone'. (4) Identity drift: founder_lock pasted verbatim, reused unchanged in shots 2 and 7. (5) Camera stacking: one dolly push-in; the visible change is her body (exhale, lean back, rub eyes). Keyframe shows her upright so the lean-back is a real 8-second change. Ends on her leaning back in the dark, which cuts cleanly to the close-up of shot 2.

Around it in a loose ring float fourteen dark glass tiles, each a simple geometric mark: ring, hexagon, chevron, node dot. **Keyframe prompt**

Fourteen dark unlit glass tiles float in a loose ring around it, each a simple geometric mark: ring, hexagon, chevron, node dot. Progression: keyframe shows only the first thread so the 8 seconds have an obvious arc. Camera: single steady rise, no rotate-plus-descend. Grade: genre auto keeps fine threads from being crushed by a heavy contrast curve. Xero/Salesforce/HubSpot are carried by VO only, never on screen. Ends on a fully lit, breathing network seen from height, cutting to the low-angle cards of shot 6 as a deliberate change of scale.

## Shot 6 — genre `drama`

**Video prompt**

Medium-wide, 40mm anamorphic, low angle in a dark volumetric space, near-black ground. Three rows of blank holographic task cards hover, small translucent rounded rectangles glowing desaturated amber, each with one abstract status dot, no writing. Over eight seconds the cards flip one after another, left to right, each settling to cool green with a soft pulse, until every card is green. Behind them a single thin thread of electric cyan light climbs steadily up and to the right, leaving a soft trail, no axes, no ticks. Fine cyan neural-mesh particles stream upward through the frame. One slow lateral dolly right, tracking the wave of flips. Cyan key, royal-blue glow, violet fill. Momentum, controlled confidence, premium. No text, no numbers.

**Keyframe prompt**

Cinematic still, 40mm anamorphic, medium-wide, low angle, dark volumetric space, near-black ground. Three rows of blank holographic task cards hover, small translucent rounded rectangles glowing desaturated amber, each with one abstract status dot, no writing; the first card at far left has just flipped to cool green with a soft pulse. Behind them a single thin thread of electric cyan light begins low at the left, no axes. Fine cyan neural-mesh particles hang in the air. Cyan key, royal-blue glow, violet fill. Premium, controlled. No text, no numbers.

**Risks**

Task cards are the highest text-garble risk in the film: guards are 'blank', 'one abstract status dot, no writing', closing 'no text, no numbers', and a keyframe locking the cards as empty rectangles. The graph may spawn axes, ticks or numerals: 'single thin thread', 'no axes, no ticks'. Amber is off-palette and may bloom orange: 'desaturated amber' plus cyan key keeps it a muted before-state; pull it down further in grade. Flips may fire all at once: 'one after another, left to right' plus a dolly right tracking the wave. Drama over action avoids handheld shake. Ends on all-green cards with the thread at its highest point and particles rising, a settled, resolved frame that cuts cleanly to the calm dawn of shot 7. If labels appear, regenerate from the keyframe rather than adding negatives.
